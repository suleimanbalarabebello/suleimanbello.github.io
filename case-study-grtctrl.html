02 — Brand Identity

The Crest

The GRTCTRL visual identity draws from baroque and rococo aesthetics, creating a luxury fashion brand rooted in ornate classical imagery. The evolution from the original Horizon wordmark to the final crest logo marked a strategic shift toward heritage luxury positioning.

The crest features intricate scrollwork, angelic figures, and royal crown motifs—establishing a visual language that communicates craftsmanship, exclusivity, and timeless elegance. The ornate detailing translates across applications from embroidered apparel to large-scale marketing, maintaining recognizability at any scale.

Final crest design featuring baroque ornamental elements

Logo Evolution

The brand identity began with a clean Horizon-font wordmark before evolving into the ornate crest system. This transition elevated the brand's market positioning, creating immediate visual impact that differentiates GRTCTRL in the independent fashion space.

03 — Design System

Visual Foundation

Color Palette

Monochromatic foundation in black, white, and grey creates versatile application across materials. The high-contrast palette works for screen printing, embroidery, digital platforms, and packaging. Silver and metallic accents enhance the luxury positioning when appropriate for premium product lines.

Typography

Originally built on Horizon font for the wordmark before transitioning to the crest-based system. The ornamental logo serves as the primary brand mark, with supporting typography kept clean and minimal to balance the elaborate central motif.

Applications

The identity system scales seamlessly across all brand touchpoints: woven labels and swing tags, apparel prints and embroidery, packaging and mailers, website and social media, marketing materials and event installations. Every application maintains consistent brand recognition.

Brand Guidelines

Comprehensive usage guidelines ensure consistency across 200+ assets. Documentation covers logo placement, minimum sizes, clear space requirements, color specifications, and application do's and don'ts for maintaining brand integrity.

07 — Impact

Key Learnings

Leading GRTCTRL developed end-to-end creative project management skills, teaching me to balance creative vision with practical business constraints while maintaining quality at scale.

"Building a fashion brand from scratch taught me that great design isn't just about aesthetics—it's about creating a cohesive system that works across every touchpoint while telling a compelling story."

— Personal Reflection

Takeaways

The experience reinforced the importance of building systematic design processes and documentation. Creating reusable templates and asset libraries proved essential for maintaining consistency while producing large volumes of content across multiple mediums.

Working across brand identity, photography, video, and digital platforms demonstrated the value of cross-disciplinary skills in modern creative work. The ability to execute across these areas enables faster iteration and stronger creative vision.

Managing 200+ assets taught me to think systematically about brand architecture, file organization, and workflow optimization—skills that translate directly to professional creative and design roles.
